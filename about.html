About PixelPop

A small studio, led by a senior designer

PixelPop is a design and digital marketing studio for startups, local businesses, lifestyle and food brands. Based in India, remote-first, and working with brands across the country.

How we work

01

Discover: We start by listening

A short brief about your business, audience and goals. No design without understanding the full picture first.

02

Strategise: Direction before pixels

We turn what we learned into a creative direction: mood, colour, type and tone of voice.

03

Design: Where it takes shape

We craft concepts, show them in real contexts, and refine them with your feedback until the work is unmistakably yours.

04

Deliver: Ready to use, yours to keep

Final files in every format, organised with a usage guide so you are always in control.
