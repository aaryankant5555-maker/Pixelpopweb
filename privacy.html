Privacy policy

What we collect when you contact us, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data PixelPop ("we", "us") collects through pixelpop-designs.vercel.app, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We keep it short and only collect what we need to reply to you and do the work you ask for.

Who we are

PixelPop is a design and digital marketing studio based in India. For anything about your data, email hello@pixelpop.studio.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, business or brand name (optional), the service you are interested in (optional), your message (optional), and the address of the page you sent it from.
  • If you message or email us: whatever you choose to send, along with your phone number or email address.
  • If you become a client: the details needed to agree a quote, deliver the project and invoice you.

We do not ask for sensitive personal data, and please don't send it.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To deliver and support a project you have agreed with us.
  • To keep records we are required to keep, such as invoices.

We use your details only for these purposes. We do not sell your data, and we will not add you to a marketing list unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

We use a few trusted services to run the website and receive enquiries. They process data on our behalf:

  • Google (Google Sheets and Apps Script): stores a copy of each contact form enquiry.
  • Web3Forms: delivers each contact form enquiry to our email inbox.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): only if you choose to message us on WhatsApp.
  • Vercel: hosts this website and keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: deliver the fonts and animation libraries this website uses. Like any website your browser loads files from, they receive your IP address.

Some of these providers may store data outside India. We would also share data if the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies, and it does not use analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to reply and follow up. Enquiries that don't lead to a project are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for the length of the project and as long as tax and accounting law requires. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, or withdraw your consent to us using it;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you.

Email hello@pixelpop.studio with your request or any complaint. We will reply as quickly as we can, and within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

This website is served over HTTPS, and we limit access to enquiries to the people who need them. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable care to protect yours.

Children

Our services are for businesses. This website is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
