Pricing FAQ

How much does a logo and brand identity cost in India with PixelPop?

Our Starter plan starts at ₹25K per project. It includes brand discovery and a logo (3 concepts), a colour palette and typography, brand guidelines as a PDF, a social profile kit with mockups, 2 rounds of revisions, and the source files with full ownership. You get a final quote after a short call.

How much does a website design cost?

Website design comes in our Growth plan, from ₹75K per project. It includes a full brand identity system, complete brand guidelines, a mobile-ready 5-page website design, on-page SEO basics, a social media template pack, stationery and an email signature, and 3 rounds of revisions. Domain and hosting are billed separately at cost.

Do you offer monthly social media plans?

Yes. Monthly plans start at ₹10K a month for one platform: 10 designed posts, a content calendar with captions, an Instagram profile clean-up and a monthly performance report. From ₹20K a month you get 12 designed posts and 4 Reels, posting and scheduling, Google Business Profile setup and updates, a quarterly competitor review, and a monthly report with a strategy call.

How many revisions do I get?

2 rounds of revisions on the Starter plan and 3 rounds on the Growth plan. Custom projects are scoped to what you need.

Do I own the final designs?

Yes. You get the source files and full ownership of all final files.

Are there costs that aren't included in the price?

Domain and hosting for websites, and ad spend for campaigns, are billed separately at cost. Everything else in a plan is listed on the pricing page.
