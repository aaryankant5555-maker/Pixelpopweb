Brand identity FAQ

How much does a brand identity cost?

The Starter brand kit starts at ₹25K per project: logo (3 concepts), colour palette and typography, brand guidelines PDF, social profile kit with mockups, 2 rounds of revisions, and source files with full ownership. The Growth plan, from ₹75K, adds a full identity system, complete guidelines and a 5-page website design.

Compare plans

How long does a brand identity take?

About 2 weeks for the Starter brand kit. The Growth plan (brand + website) takes about 4 weeks.

Which files will I get?

Your logo as SVG and PNG, a brand guidelines PDF, a social media kit, your fonts and colours, and a usage guide, organised in one folder. You own all final files, including the source files.

How many logo concepts and revisions are included?

3 logo concepts on the Starter plan, with 2 rounds of revisions. The Growth plan includes 3 rounds of revisions.

Can you also design my website and social media?

Yes. The Growth plan combines your brand identity with a mobile-ready 5-page website design and a social media template pack, and our monthly plans keep your social media running after launch.
