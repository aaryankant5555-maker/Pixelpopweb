Social media FAQ

How much does social media management cost?

Monthly plans start at ₹10K a month for one platform, and ₹20K a month for posts, Reels and local presence. Bigger, multi-platform plans are quoted after a short call.

See full pricing

What do I get for ₹10K a month?

10 designed posts a month, a content calendar with captions, an Instagram profile clean-up and a monthly performance report, for one platform.

Do you make Reels?

Yes. The ₹20K monthly plan includes 4 Reels a month alongside 12 designed posts, and Reels editing is also available as part of custom plans.

Will you post for us?

Yes. Posting and scheduling are included from the ₹20K monthly plan.

Can you set up our Google Business Profile?

Yes. Google Business Profile setup and updates are included in the ₹20K monthly plan, so nearby customers can find you and call.

Do you run influencer campaigns?

Yes, on request. We shortlist local creators whose audience matches your customers, agree what each creator will post in writing, run the campaign and report the results.

Is ad spend included?

No. Ad spend is billed separately at cost.
